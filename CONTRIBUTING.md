# Contributing to Spellcross Reloaded

Spellcross Reloaded welcomes fixes, improvements and testing. For new project-owned source contributions, CPAL-1.0 is the applicable license (see LICENSE and LICENSING.md). By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you agree to license the rights you are authorized to grant under CPAL-1.0. Do not submit code, assets or material you do not have the right to contribute.

For a new covered source file, copy the Exhibit A notice from source/project_attribution.h (under spellcross-remaster-main), retain the copyright and attribution information, and add your contributor name when appropriate. For project-owned modifications to existing files, preserve the original license and copyright notices, identify the new CPAL-covered portions and include the Exhibit A notice. Third-party imports must retain their licenses. Document changes and dates in CHANGES.md or the published dated change history. Previously published MIT grants are not withdrawn.

Keep the About attribution, README acknowledgments and third-party notices consistent. Do not add commercial retail game assets.
