# Spellcross Reloaded v0.9.2.2

A maintenance release for the original Spellcross campaign on modern Windows. This update improves tactical battle stability, counter-attack deployment, AI behavior and visibility of troops near battlefield boundaries.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Strategic defence and counter-attacks:** defensive deployments use the Alliance start positions authored in the original DTA map data, avoiding missions with too few deployment squares. Mission definitions also load correctly with either uppercase `.DEF` or lowercase `.def` filenames. ([#58](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/58), [#59](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/59))
- **Battlefield ambience:** the tactical simulation and ambient sound loops stop while the main menu or strategic view is visible; ambience resumes when returning to the battlefield. ([#60](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/60))
- **Melee combat:** range-one units (including Magotar) no longer receive downhill elevation range bonuses that allowed attacks from several tiles away. ([#61](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/61))
- **Enemy AI:** attacked enemy groups can reconsider their orders even when they do not currently have direct line of sight to the attacker. Reaching a non-combat movement destination no longer sends units on pointless adjacent-tile excursions. ([#62](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/62))
- **Unit placement safety:** fixed a boundary-case out-of-bounds memory access during the nearest-free-tile search. ([#63](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/63))
- **Full battlefield navigation:** the tactical camera can scroll to the actual map edges, where valid units can arrive through group movement or enemy AI. ([#64](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/pull/64))

## Download and installation

Choose either **`Spellcross_Reloaded_v0.9.2.2.zip`** (portable, extract to a writable folder) or **`Spellcross_Reloaded_v0.9.2.2.exe`** (WinRAR self-extracting installer with selectable location). Both packages contain `Spellcross.exe` and the standalone map editor.

Launch `Spellcross.exe` and follow the data import wizard if this is your first installation. Your original Czech or English Spellcross game data is required and **is not included** in the download. On a fresh Windows system, the [Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64)](https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe) may also be needed.

This release does not introduce a new tactical save format. As a precaution, back up your existing saves and imported game data before installing over an older build.
