# Spellcross Reloaded v0.9.4.2

Windows x64 maintenance release. Changes below are relative to [v0.9.4.1](https://github.com/luboshorak/spellcross-reloaded/releases/tag/v0.9.4.1).

## Hierarchy and unit indicators

- Restore original hierarchy mouse editing: left-click picks/places, right-click removes. The roster scrolls through companies and commanders on both pages, and companies can be arranged before assigning a commander. Removing or replacing a host company clears its commander-host assignments consistently. Formation bonuses and membership survive strategic and tactical saves. (#99)
- Restore the original 27×3 strategic strength bars and the four retail activity icons for units returning from combat, purchased, recruited/replenished, or upgraded/rearmed. Icons reflect actual operation state and persist through saves. Existing saves with an unknown cooldown reason do not receive an invented icon. These indicators are restored in Hierarchy, Units/support lists and Buy; the mission-selection list is unchanged. (#99)
- Correct fractional health for single-member units in the tactical HUD, overhead display and unit information. Multi-member strength and wounded counts retain their existing meaning. (#99)

## Combat and commander losses

- Classical artillery cannot fire from sloped tiles when its original FIRE_NOT_SLOPES flag forbids it. Mortar infantry and M106 retain their original exceptions; elevated flat tiles remain valid. The same rule applies to player attacks, AI and defensive fire. (#99)
- Announce lost allied formation commanders by name in the existing tactical information window, using original CZ/EN casualty text. Multiple officers hosted by one company queue individually, including loss of the final allied company before game over. Reloading after their loss does not repeat the messages. Existing morale shocks, formation downgrades and save formats are preserved. Exact original punctuation/rank placement and presentation timing remain documented verification limits. (#102)

## Videos and campaign transitions

- Mute mission background sounds while either original video playback or MP4 fallback is active. Video narration stays audible. Completion, skipping, destruction and startup failure restore the current user volume without overwriting settings. (#103, #101)
- Show strategic event messages, including reinforcement announcements, only after the strategic map is visible and painted and transition videos have finished. Event actions keep their existing timing; messages and reinforcements are not duplicated. (#104)

## Packages

- `Spellcross_Reloaded_v0.9.4.2.zip` — portable game and standalone map editor.
- `Spellcross_Reloaded_v0.9.4.2.exe` — WinRAR SFX installer, when built with WinRAR.
- Windows FileVersion / ProductVersion: **0.9.4.2**.
- Display/package label: **0.9.4.2**; Git tag: **v0.9.4.2**.

Original commercial game archives are not bundled. Czech and English editions are supported; Polish and Italian retail-data loading remains experimental. Keep a backup of saves when updating.

## Validation and build handoff

Automated checks cover release metadata/version ordering and existing campaign rules. Native regressions cover hierarchy controls and save round trips, formation bonuses and commander losses, retail indicator pixels and health states, artillery/mortar terrain rules, video PCM mixing, strategic-message ownership/first paint, temporary companies and recapture lifecycle.

Build packages from the final reviewed source using `packaging/Build-CombinedRelease.ps1 -Rebuild`. The script reads this version from `source/app_version.h` and verifies the compiled executable version.

Before publishing, smoke-test the extracted game and standalone editor, CZ/EN loading, Continue from strategic/tactical saves, hierarchy bonuses, wounded officers, artillery/mortars, video completion/skipping and reinforcement messages after entering the strategic map. Packages must include the 37 manifest-verified fallback videos and freshly compiled executables.
